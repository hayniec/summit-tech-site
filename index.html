Who We Are

Summit Technologies, Inc. is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) headquartered in Panama City Beach, Florida. Founded in 1992, we have built a solid reputation for delivering highly reliable, agile, and cost-effective solutions tailored to the operational demands of our clients.

We hold an approved DCAA-compliant government accounting system and an accepted costing policy, enabling us to execute cost-reimbursement, time-and-materials, and fixed-price contracts efficiently.

Our Accessibility Commitment

Summit Technologies, Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards, complying with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act and W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Conformance Status

This website is fully conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards. Full conformance means that the content fully conforms to the accessibility standard without exceptions.

Accessibility Measures Implemented

  • Keyboard Accessibility: All functional elements are completely operable via keyboard interface with clear visible focus rings.
  • Semantic Structure: Proper landmark roles, heading hierarchy, and list tags are embedded to assist screen reader users.
  • Color & Contrast: Text elements maintain contrast ratios exceeding the 4.5:1 requirement.
  • Reduced Motion: Supports system settings for users with motion sensitivity (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
  • Text Equivalents: Meaningful alt text is provided for informative visual assets.
